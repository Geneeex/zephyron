# Figure provenance

The manuscript contains **38 numbered figures and 40 embedded image files**. The table maps every embedded file to its repository source. Files in the manuscript LaTeX assets folder are byte-identical copies of the indicated source images; duplicate filenames support a self-contained Overleaf project. Source hashes are retained in the manuscript asset manifest and in [third_party_licenses.json](../third_party_licenses.json) for reused literature content.

## Evidence categories

- **Photographs:** three author-supplied rover photographs, retained without pixel modification. They document visible prototype structure; dimensions, payload, performance, and field-test results are not inferred from photographs.
- **Blender:** nine editable mission scenes in [Zephyron_Research_Missions.blend](../research/blender/Zephyron_Research_Missions.blend), with renders M01.png through M09.png. They are design illustrations rendered from Blender, not photographs, empirical mission records, or evidence of successful machine learning.
- **Canva:** fifteen native diagrams created using editable text, boxes, lines, and arrowheads, imported into an editable Canva design and rendered by Canva. The manuscript uses the native Canva page renders, including figure titles and arrow legends. They are not AI-generated raster substitutes.
- **Analytical graphs:** nine graphs generated from declared engineering assumptions and executable calculations. They show model scenarios, not physical Zephyron measurements.
- **Published figures:** six original image files from four papers, occupying manuscript Figures 2–6. Their source authors, licenses, and modifications are in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Canva master and resolution

The [Canva master view](https://www.canva.com/d/IDTTD9Ce011n5H1) is provided for inspection. Editing access remains with the owner; no edit-access link or signed download URL is published here. The included genuine native previews are **596 × 335 pixels**. They retain titles and legends but are provisional publication assets. Higher-resolution native exports should replace them before a publication requires larger figures. Local HTML renderings, if regenerated for inspection, must not be described as Canva exports.
